# Fondest Memories
_Some places feel familiar. Some memories feel real. And some things are better left forgotten._

Have you ever wandered through a place that felt like you've been there before, yet couldn't remember when?

**Fondest Memories** is a liminal space mod that takes you somewhere beyond the boundaries of the familiar. Explore endless corridors, abandoned rooms, forgotten playgrounds, and places that seem to exist somewhere between dreams and reality.

There's something strangely comforting about these places. The quiet splashes of water, the distant sound of something you can't quite identify, the feeling that you've returned to somewhere you once knew.

But something isn't quite right.

The deeper you wander, the more you'll begin to question where you are, how you got here, and whether you've ever truly left.

Perhaps these places are fragments of forgotten memories. Perhaps they're remnants of memories that never existed.

Or perhaps...

You've been here before.

And you will be here again.

### Shader Support
This mod does in fact work with Iris and Sodium. But support for other rendering mods is unlikely.

### Dependencies
This mod depends on Fabric API and Veyl.
